Case-based simulation for clinical reasoning

The patient keeps getting worse until you act.

Each case runs in real time. Take a history, examine, order tests and treat. The monitor responds to what you do and what you don’t do. At the end you get a scored debrief with the timeline of your decisions and teaching points.
